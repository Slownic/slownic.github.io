Level Up Runner

RoleGame & Level Designer
StudioApp Ideas Games
GenreHyper-Casual Runner

1.0 — Overview

My first published game!

Published in 2021 by Lion Studios, Level Up Runner is an hyper-casual runner developed together with App Ideas Games, and it reached more than 15M players worldwide.

This is where I learned what it means to work with a publisher on a day-to-day basis, and on a very short iteration time on top of that!

I've designed and built pretty much all the levels, along with all the monetization and engagement systems in the game.
