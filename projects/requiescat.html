Requiescat: The Dreadful

RoleFounder & Game Developer
GenreIndie Survival Horror
Timeline2025 – Present
StatusPlayable Demo

1.0 — Overview

Founded and drove development of Requiescat: The Dreadful, an indie survival-horror title, taking it from concept through a fully playable demo. Being the founder of a small project meant covering the full pipeline — not just designing systems, but building the world they live in.

2.0 — Role Breakdown

Level Design & Lighting Artist

Designed and lit the demo's environments, shaping map layouts and atmosphere to reinforce the game's horror tone.

Technical Game Designer

Tuned core mechanics and systems for pacing and difficulty, balancing gameplay across the full demo build.

QA

Tracked and resolved issues across QA passes, keeping builds stable through to delivery.

Narrative Designer

Wrote the game's texts and dialogue, shaping tone and voice for its narrative.
