Mini Monsters: Card Collector

RoleTechnical Game Designer
StudioApp Ideas Games
GenreCard / Collection
StatusPublished

1.0 — Overview

A TCG battle game built with App Ideas Games and published by HOMA Games in 2024.

It was an extremely entertaining challenge designing the battle system, as well as creating and balancing the 300+ cards we had in the game, the Game Economy and Retention Systems we included.

I've met some true talent while working with HOMA, and I'm deeply grateful for all the mind-widening lessons I learned along the way.
